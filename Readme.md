Disaster-related aerial imagery has predominantly been studied in the context of emergency response, with existing datasets and approaches focusing on damage assessment, affected-area mapping, and the identification of structures requiring assistance. In contrast, the potential of aerial imagery for post-disaster environmental assessment and waste management has received considerably less attention. In particular, there is, to the best of our knowledge, a lack of publicly available datasets specifically designed to support the identification and classification of materials in disaster-generated debris according to their potential for recycling and recovery.
This work addresses this complementary perspective by introducing a dataset focused on material identification in aerial imagery of disaster-affected areas, we present a lightweight approach RecyMat with the broader objective of supporting post-disaster waste characterization and environmentally informed recycling and recovery strategies.
This repository provides an anonymized sample of the dataset introduced in our submission. The sample is intended to give reviewers an overview of the dataset's content, structure, and diversity, and to facilitate the assessment of its relevance to the proposed research.
The complete dataset and the model for material identification will be made publicly available to the research community following publication of the paper.
